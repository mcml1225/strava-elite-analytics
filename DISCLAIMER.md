\## Strava Elite Athletes Analytics Platform (SEAAP)



\### Data Access Limitation



This project is a \*\*demonstration of data science techniques and architecture\*\*. It does \*\*not\*\* access or collect real training data from elite athletes without authorization.



\### Strava API Restrictions



According to Strava's API Terms of Service:



> "You may only access data that the user has explicitly authorized you to access. You may not attempt to scrape or otherwise access data that is not explicitly authorized."



This means:

\- ❌ You cannot view another athlete's heart rate data without their permission

\- ❌ You cannot view another athlete's power data without their permission

\- ❌ You cannot view another athlete's detailed activities without their permission

\- ✅ You can only view data from athletes who have authorized your application
